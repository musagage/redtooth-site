Future of Communication

What Redtooth Wide-Network (RWN) does is fundamentally different from traditional internet-based communication. It basically sends information as a wireless signal transmission to a "network relay."

The relay is a device that amplifies this transmission signal and passes it down to different nodes at greater distances wirelessly and offline.

Through continuous signal propagation, the websites or any applications on the local machine (Primary Node) of the developer can be displayed and interacted with negligible latency.

Older generation nodes may need an external device connected (through port) and its specific driver to be able to read that signal. However, the vision is for Redtooth to become a universal protocol that works across all devices without specialized hardware requirements.
