What Redtooth Is

Redtooth is a hardware agnostic communication protocol that can help in data transfer all over the world, wirelessly and offline.

Redtooth aims to bypass some of the real barriers to allow for smooth data transfer and retrieval across greater distances. These barriers include:

  • Cloud infrastructure
  • Network availability
  • Latency
  • Geo-restrictions on otherwise standard transfer protocols and methods

Redtooth is not just a non-typical data protocol but also a scaffold for potentially a new informational exchange platform.

Unlike traditional communication methods, Redtooth operates independently of hardware constraints, enabling seamless data transfer across vast distances without the need for specialized chips or embedded hardware components.
