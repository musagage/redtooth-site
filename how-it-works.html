How It Works

SMS and MMS messages can only be used on mobile phones. They are not normally sent or received through computers or laptops, because this type of messaging depends mainly on the phone's SIM card. In addition, SMS and MMS cannot support live streaming or any kind of interactive, real-time media. They also cannot be used to share a webpage from a local device to a wide, "global" audience without an internet connection.

Bluetooth also depends on hardware to work. It can only send and receive data wirelessly and offline within a short range, usually between 10 and 100 meters. For a device to use Bluetooth, it must have a Bluetooth chip built into it. Devices without this chip, such as older desktop computers, need other methods, like an Ethernet cable or an external receiver connected through a USB port.

Redtooth, however, does not depend on any special chip or hardware. It works as software, which allows it to send and receive files over much longer distances, without the internet and without wires. In practice, it has been tested over 272 kilometers, and in theory, it could work over several thousand kilometers.
